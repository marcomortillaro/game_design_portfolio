### The Project
Soulstice is a fast-paced AA Action/Hack 'n' Slash title where players control two sisters simultaneously. As the **Lead Game Designer**, I was responsible for the core gameplay vision and managing a team of 6 designers throughout the full production cycle.

### My Role: Combat & 3Cs Specialist
My primary focus was defining the **Combat System** and the **3Cs** (Character, Controls, Camera) to ensure a high-degree of responsiveness and "game feel."

* **Combat Design:** Oversaw the development of the dual-character combat mechanics, weapon balancing, and synergy between Briar and Lute.
* **Encounter Design:** Designed and balanced high-stakes boss fights and enemy AI behaviors to maintain a challenging yet fair difficulty curve.
* **Team Leadership:** Managed the design pipeline, ensuring feature consistency and cross-department communication between art, animation, and programming.
* **Systems Balancing:** Fine-tuned the progression systems and combat economy to support a 20+ hour experience.

> "Translating a complex dual-protagonist concept into a fluid, high-speed combat experience was the core challenge of this project."
