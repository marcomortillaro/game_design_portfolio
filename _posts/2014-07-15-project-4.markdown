### THE PROJECT
Since 2022, I have been serving as the **Lead Game Designer** on a major unannounced production. While currently under NDA, the project represents a significant evolution in the studio's portfolio, blending high-end action with complex systemic design.

### MY ROLE: DESIGN LEADERSHIP & SYSTEMS
In this role, I am responsible for overseeing the overall design vision, specifically focusing on the synergy between action combat and persistent progression systems.

* **Roguelike Systems Design**: Architecting procedural gameplay loops, meta-progression systems, and randomized encounter logic to ensure high replayability.
* **Team Management**: Leading and mentoring a core design team of 6 people, ensuring consistency across all gameplay systems.
* **Combat & 3Cs**: Maintaining the studio's high standards for character responsiveness while adapting 3Cs to the unpredictable nature of roguelike environments.
* **Balancing & Economy**: Overseeing the mathematical balancing of items, abilities, and difficulty curves essential for a fair yet challenging roguelike experience.

> "Developing a Roguelike of this scale requires a perfect balance between procedural randomness and tight, intentional combat design."
