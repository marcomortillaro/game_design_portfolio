### THE PROJECT
Theseus is a dark, cinematic reimagining of the Minotaur myth, specifically designed for Virtual Reality. As a **Game Designer** (and later **Lead**), I focused on creating a third-person VR experience that felt visceral without compromising player comfort.

### MY ROLE: LEAD GAME DESIGNER
I stepped into the Lead role during production, ensuring the project met high-quality standards for VR gameplay and narrative flow.

* **VR Camera & 3Cs**: Designed and tuned a proprietary third-person camera system specifically for VR to minimize motion sickness while maintaining a cinematic feel.
* **Combat in VR**: Developed combat mechanics that translate well to the VR space, focusing on scale and spatial awareness during encounters with the Minotaur.
* **Leadership Transition**: Successfully took over the design leadership mid-project, managing the final polishing phase and balancing the core gameplay loop.
* **Immersive Systems**: Bridged the gap between traditional action mechanics and the unique constraints of VR hardware (PC/PS4).

> "Mastering the third-person perspective in VR required a deep dive into player comfort and spatial storytelling."
