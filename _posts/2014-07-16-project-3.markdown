### THE PROJECT
Joe Dever's Lone Wolf is an innovative project that merges a traditional "Choose Your Own Adventure" gamebook with a full 3D turn-based combat system. Working on such a prestigious IP required a deep respect for the source material and a focus on narrative-driven mechanics.

### MY ROLE: GAMEBOOK DESIGNER
My work focused on the intersection of storytelling and gameplay, ensuring that the player's choices felt impactful and consistent with the Lone Wolf universe.

* **Narrative Systems**: Designed the branching narrative paths and the integration of RPG stats into the text-based gameplay.
* **Systems Design**: Developed the "Gamebook" logic, balancing the difficulty of non-combat encounters and character progression.
* **Multi-platform Delivery**: Contributed to the design adjustments for Mobile, PC, and Console releases (PS4, Xbox One and Nintendo Switch), ensuring a seamless experience across all devices.
* **IP Collaboration**: Worked within the constraints of Joe Dever's world-renowned fantasy setting, maintaining the tone and lore that fans expect.

> "Merging old-school interactive storytelling with modern RPG combat was a masterclass in hybrid system design."
