### THE OVERVIEW
Before specializing in the Action AA space, I developed a strong foundation in **Edutainment and Mobile UX**. Between my experience at BeeCreative, Reply, and as an independent developer, I designed and delivered dozens of titles focused on intuitive gameplay and accessibility.

### KEY HIGHLIGHTS

* **Chicco Happy Tab**: Played a key role in the UX/UI design of the official Android tablet for kids. I designed the custom launcher and the core educational suite, ensuring a safe, "frictionless" interface for toddlers.
* **Corporate Edutainment (Ferrero, Mulino Bianco)**: Designed interactive experiences and "advergaming" solutions for major Italian brands, balancing marketing goals with engaging gameplay loops.
* **Indie Development (Animal's Boat for Toddlers)**: Developed and published independent titles, managing the full lifecycle from core design to store optimization. *Animal's Boat* became a benchmark for my "zero-text" design philosophy.
* **UX for Non-Readers**: Mastered the art of designing for audiences that cannot read, using visual hierarchy, audio cues, and haptic feedback as the primary language.

> "Working for brands like Chicco and Ferrero taught me the importance of brand consistency, while indie development gave me the technical grit to ship products independently."
